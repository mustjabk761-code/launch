About Product Launch Directory

Product Launch Directory is a free launchpad for makers of web tools, apps and side projects. We built it because many small makers have a great tool but nowhere simple to show it. Here you can list your product in about two minutes, with no account and no password.

What we do

Makers submit their tools. Visitors browse the newest launches, upvote the ones they like and leave comments. Good products rise in the Top view, and every product has its own link that you can share.

Our principles

  • Free and simple. No sign-up and no fee to launch.
  • Honest. Links from the directory are nofollow. We do not sell rankings and we do not promise SEO results.
  • Privacy first. Your email is never shown publicly. It is used only to send you your Edit Key.
  • Clean listings. Spam, copied and broken listings are removed. Visitors can report any product.

Who runs it

Product Launch Directory is a project by Global Tools Box, a free collection of web tools. We read every report and message ourselves.
