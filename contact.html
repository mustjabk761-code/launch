Contact Us

We are happy to hear from you. Send us an email and we will reply as soon as we can, usually within a few days.

Email: globaltoolsboxcontact@gmail.com

What you can write to us about

  • Help with your listing or a lost Edit Key.
  • Request to remove or correct a product.
  • Report of spam, copied content or a broken link. You can also use the Report button on any product card.
  • Feedback and ideas to improve the directory.
  • Business or partnership questions.

Tips for a faster reply

  • Write the name of your product and its website address.
  • Use the same email address you used when you launched the product.
  • Never send your Edit Key in an email unless we ask for it to verify ownership.

Please do not send unsolicited promotions. We do not sell links, rankings or reviews.
