123audio

Convert audio and video to accurate, editable text online with AI-powered transcription.

Category: AI Tools | Listed: 2026-11-26

123audio is a free online audio to text converter that turns audio and video into accurate, editable text. It uses AI speech recognition to create reviewable transcripts with automatic timestamps and speaker labels. Guests can start without an account. Exports include TXT, DOCX, PDF, and SRT or VTT subtitles, plus on-demand summaries and translations. Free tier is available, along with paid plans.
