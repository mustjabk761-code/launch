gridtabs

Turn your new tab into a clean grid of your favorite sites

Category: Productivity | Tags: #Chrome extension #new tab #bookmarks #productivity | Listed: 2026-11-27

gridtabs is a speed dial new tab extension and a visual bookmark manager for Chrome and Edge. It turns your new tab into a clean grid of favorite sites, plus small app tiles (to-dos, notes, weather), a cell pet (Momo), and an agent that can make cells. Free plan: 30 cells. Pro is US$39.90/year.
