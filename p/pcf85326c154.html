AgePaws

Pet age calculator for dogs, cats and kittens

Category: Productivity | Tags: #pet age calculator #dog years #cat years #pets | Listed: 2026-11-22

AgePaws is a free pet age calculator for dog and cat owners. Enter your pet's age (plus adult size for dogs, indoor/outdoor for cats, or months for kittens) and it returns the human-year equivalent together with the current life stage and the next one. Dog, cat and kitten calculators sit alongside dog-years and cat-years charts from 1 to 20. Estimates only, not veterinary advice. No sign-up.
