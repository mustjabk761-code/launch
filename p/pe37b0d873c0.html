LinkSave

A Chrome extension that puts a bookmark next to Send on every LinkedIn post, and a Saved tab in the

Category: Productivity | Listed: 2026-11-23

I save a lot of posts on LinkedIn (hiring threads, good write-ups, things to read later), and it always takes three clicks: open the ⋯ menu, find Save, close the menu. On some posts I'd lose my place in the feed doing it. A Chrome extension that puts a bookmark next to Send on every LinkedIn post, and a Saved tab in the top bar. Uses LinkedIn’s own Save. Get it: https://atikbuilds.com/linksave
