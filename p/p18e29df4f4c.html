DisplaySwitcher

Switch your monitor's video input and transfer text and files between computers.

Category: Productivity | Listed: 2026-11-17

DisplaySwitcher is a Linux, macOS and Windows desktop app that switches your monitor's video input with one click instead of the physical monitor buttons. It works with monitors that support DDC/CI, and it also transfers text and files between your computers over encrypted connections.
