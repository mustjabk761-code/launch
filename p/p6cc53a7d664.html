Gamepad Bench

Free browser tester for stick drift, buttons, triggers and polling rate.

Category: Developer Tools | Tags: #gamepad #controller #tester #hardware | Listed: 2026-11-12

Gamepad Bench is a free browser tool for testing USB and Bluetooth controllers. Connect a DualSense, Xbox pad or Switch Pro to inspect stick centering, trigger travel, buttons, rumble and polling rate with no install. Useful before a repair or when checking a hall-effect module.
