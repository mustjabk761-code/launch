ASL Translator

Free text to ASL fingerspelling translator

Category: Education | Listed: 2026-11-22

Free, no-signup tool that converts English text (A-Z, up to 200 chars) into American Sign Language fingerspelling, showing each letter handshape. Includes A-Z practice, an ASL dictionary and videos of common signs. Spells letters only; no full ASL sentence translation.
