SpeechCalc

Free browser tool that estimates speech time from a word count

Category: Productivity | Listed: 2026-11-27

SpeechCalc is a free, no-account browser tool for estimating speech time. Paste a draft or enter a word count to convert words to minutes, or a target duration for a word budget. Use 100, 130, or 160 wpm as illustrative settings, or your own pace. Planning estimates, not recordings. Does not upload or save your script.
