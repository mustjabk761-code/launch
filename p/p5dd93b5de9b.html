Revolver Tab

Rotate tabs automatically. All day.

Category: Productivity | Tags: #Chrome extension #dashboard #kiosk #productivity | Listed: 2026-11-26

Revolver Tab is a free Chrome extension that automatically rotates and refreshes tabs for dashboards, kiosks and office TVs. Choose your open tabs or a playlist of URLs, set the interval, and press Start. Display dashboards, Google Sheets, Slides and Excel Online, and keep going after crashes, updates and power cuts.
