Save Image As Type

Right-click to save images as JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF or GIF

Category: Productivity | Tags: #chrome extension #image converter #productivity #design | Listed: 2026-11-25

Save Image As Type is a free Chrome extension that adds a right-click menu to convert and save any web image as JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF or GIF. Turn a WebP or AVIF into a JPG or PNG in one click, with adjustable quality. Everything runs locally in your browser: no uploads, no account, and no watermark.
