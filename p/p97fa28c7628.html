ICS to CSV

Turn an exported calendar into a spreadsheet, in your browser

Category: Productivity | Tags: #calendar #ics #csv #excel | Listed: 2026-11-21

Turn an exported .ics calendar into a CSV for Excel or Google Sheets: summary, start, end, timezone, duration, location, description, recurrence and raw exception columns. Each event is one row: recurring events are not expanded into repetitions, and moved or cancelled occurrences are not applied. Runs in your browser; the file is never uploaded. Free, no sign-up.
