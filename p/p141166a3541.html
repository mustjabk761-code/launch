MorseAtlas

Text ↔ Morse. Play, WAV, flash — all local.

Category: Education | Tags: #morse code #translator #learning #offline | Listed: 2026-11-13

MorseAtlas is a free in-browser Morse code translator. Paste text or dots/dashes (auto-detect), hear tones at adjustable WPM/pitch/volume, download a WAV, flash the screen, or copy a share link. All processing stays on your device; the page works offline after it has loaded.
