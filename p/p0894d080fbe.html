SongSimilar

Free similar song finder. 20 ranked tracks, no signup.

Category: Productivity | Listed: 2026-11-25

SongSimilar is a free similar song finder that works in any browser with no signup. Enter a song title and artist to get 20 similar songs ranked by listener overlap, using real listening data from Last.fm and Deezer. Each result shows a 0-100% match score, cover art and genre tags, with one-click links to YouTube, Spotify and Amazon Music. Save favorites or browse popular songs by genre and mood.
