Loop Rideshare

Canada's community carpool app — split costs fairly, never surge pricing

Category: Productivity | Listed: 2026-10-29

Loop Rideshare is Canada's community carpool app. Drivers post seats on trips they are already taking and riders book them in the app, splitting the true cost of the journey — no surge pricing, ever. Features include campus and city Circles, event rides, PIN-verified pickups, in-app payments, Loop Coins rewards, and a 20% student discount with student email verification. Available on iOS and Andro
