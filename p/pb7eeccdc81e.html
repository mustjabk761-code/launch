Classroom Noise Meter

Free classroom noise meter that calibrates to your room

Category: Education | Tags: #classroom noise meter #classroom management #teaching tools #quiet jar | Listed: 2026-11-19

Classroom Noise Meter is a free browser noise monitor teachers project in seconds. Ten quiet seconds set the room floor, then the screen shows Quiet, OK, Noisy or Too loud, words the back row can read. Scenes cover tests, independent and group work; pick a traffic light, bubbles or bouncing balls, and fill a Quiet jar. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
