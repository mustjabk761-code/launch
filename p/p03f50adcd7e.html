zippic

Compress images in your browser — files never leave your device.

Category: Design | Tags: #image compression #webp #privacy #browser tools | Listed: 2026-11-12

zippic is a free image compressor that runs entirely in the browser. Drop JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or GIF files onto the page, set quality or a target size, and download the results or a ZIP of a whole batch. Compression uses WebAssembly on your device, so originals are never uploaded. No account and no signup.
