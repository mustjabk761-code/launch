CompressAny

Compress images in your browser — free, no upload, no sign-up.

Category: Productivity | Listed: 2026-11-22

CompressAny is a free browser-based image compressor. Images stay on your device and are never uploaded. There is no account, watermark, or daily quota. It supports JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC, compresses up to 20 images per batch, can aim for a target such as 100 KB, and offers individual or ZIP downloads.
