PixRoom

Mac photo manager for local photo and video libraries

Category: Productivity | Tags: #mac #photo-manager #photo-organizer #video | Listed: 2026-11-23

PixRoom is a native macOS photo organizer for browsing, tagging, rating and searching local photo and video libraries without uploading files to the cloud. Basic organization is free; an optional $19.90 one-time upgrade adds on-device AI search. It is a manager, not a photo editor, and Mac-only.
