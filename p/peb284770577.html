Be Serious

Lock your apps. Earn them back.

Category: Productivity | Tags: #iPhone #habits #screen time #productivity | Listed: 2026-11-11

Be Serious is an iPhone habit app for iOS 18+. Lock chosen apps with Screen Time until you complete your habits and earn them back. It rewards follow-through instead of only blocking. Choose the apps that distract you, set the habits you want to keep, and those apps stay locked until you follow through. Available on the App Store.
