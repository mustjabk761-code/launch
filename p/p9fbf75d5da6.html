Xun Qiao

Follow the news. See the market.

Category: Productivity | Tags: #news #research #crypto #stocks | Listed: 2026-11-17

Xun Qiao is a free research workspace for cryptocurrency and U.S. stocks. It lines up headlines with related market context so a story, the affected assets and the price picture can be reviewed together. The homepage can be browsed without an account. Optional paid tiers exist; the core workflow stays free. It does not execute trades.
