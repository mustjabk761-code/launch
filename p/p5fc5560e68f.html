Salt Tidings

Free email alerts for your NOAA tide station, only when something happens

Category: Productivity | Tags: #free #no-signup #weather #boating | Listed: 2026-11-17

Pick one of 3,502 NOAA tide stations. Salt Tidings emails you only when the National Weather Service issues a marine or coastal alert there (small craft advisory, gale, coastal flood, rip currents, fog) or NOAA predicts an unusually high or low tide. Free, double opt-in, one-click unsubscribe. No digest, no ads.
