Remove AI Label

Clean photo & video metadata in your browser, no upload

Category: Productivity | Tags: #metadata #exif #privacy #photo | Listed: 2026-11-15

Remove AI Label is a browser-based metadata checker and cleaner for images and videos. Drop in JPG, PNG or WebP images or supported MP4/MOV files, inspect EXIF (including GPS and camera details), XMP, IPTC and PNG text chunks, choose what to keep, and download a separate clean copy. Files are not uploaded. Cleaning cannot remove platform AI labels or SynthID.
