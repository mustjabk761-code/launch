Bulk Barcode Generator

Create barcodes in bulk from Excel or CSV, download as ZIP

Category: Productivity | Tags: #barcode generator #bulk barcode #code 128 #ean-13 | Listed: 2026-11-10

Bulk Barcode Generator is a web tool for making many barcodes at once. Enter one value per line, or upload a CSV or Excel (XLSX) file. It supports CODE 128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC, EAN-5, EAN-2, CODE 39, ITF-14, MSI, Pharmacode and Codabar. Images are saved as PNG or SVG. The free plan creates up to 20 barcodes per batch; the Professional plan raises that to 1,000 per batch.
