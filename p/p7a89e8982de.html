FaceRef

3D head reference for face angles and portrait light

Category: Design | Tags: #3d head reference #portrait lighting #controlnet #drawing reference | Listed: 2026-11-19

FaceRef is a browser-based 3D head reference tool for artists and AI portrait creators. Orbit a 3D head, set exact yaw and pitch, and move the key light to study portrait shadow design. Export Loomis sketches, Asaro plane studies, and depth or edge maps for ControlNet in Stable Diffusion, FLUX and ComfyUI. Free tier, Pro from $4.99/mo.
