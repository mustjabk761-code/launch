Pollen Atlas

Free pollen counts, air quality and weather for 247,279 towns.

Category: Education | Tags: #pollen #weather #air quality #health | Listed: 2026-11-21

Pollen Atlas is a free pollen count, air quality and weather service covering 247,279 towns. Every city page shows today's Total Pollen Index (0–100) with tree, grass and weed levels, the dominant allergen and a 5-day forecast. It also publishes a national pollen heatmap, daily city rankings and a free directory of 47,033 allergists.
