YouTube Transcript Generator

Paste a video link, get a timestamped transcript in ~3 seconds

Category: Productivity | Tags: #transcript #youtube transcript #subtitle export #srt | Listed: 2026-11-19

Paste a YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or podcast link and get searchable, timestamped text in ~3 seconds. It reads the caption track already on the platform instead of re-running speech recognition, so results are fast and the wording is the uploader's own. Each paragraph has a clickable timestamp plus a search box. Export TXT free without an account; SRT, VTT and Markdown with a free account.
