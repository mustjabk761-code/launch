Why a simple launch directory still matters

We've all been there - you pour your heart into creating something, fix the last issue, and then... crickets. It's tough to get noticed on big launch platforms, especially when you're new and don't have a following. That's why we created the Product Launch Directory. It's a low-key, welcoming spot where you can list your free tool in just a few minutes, and connect with people who are excited to try out new stuff.

No need to sign up or create an account. You don't have to make a profile, confirm your phone number, or wait for someone to approve you. It's simple: just copy and paste your website's address, take a look at the details, and hit launch. That's it. We like it this way and we're going to keep it like that.

Who this directory is for

We're looking for people like you - the ones who create stuff like calculators, converters, AI helpers, design tools, and study aids. If you're a solo developer, a student, or a freelancer, you're in the right place. We also welcome indie hackers and small teams. You don't need a big company or a fancy logo to join us. Just give us a simple name, a genuine tagline, and a link that works, and you're all set.

Visitors are welcome too. We've got a bunch of handy tools that don't require you to sign up. You can check out the latest additions, look for stuff by category, and save your favorites. And the best part? It's all free.

How to launch your product in five steps

Click the Submit button at the top of the page, then follow these steps. You can find more info in our launch guide.

  • Paste your website URL first. Add your website's URL, then tap the Auto-fill button. It looks at your site's public title and description and suggests a name, a short tagline, and a logo. Don't worry, it only fills in the blanks, so anything you've already written stays the same.
  • Check every detail. Auto-fill is a helper, so give its suggestions a quick read. If something doesn't sound right, fix it. And make sure the tagline sounds like something a real person would actually say.
  • Select a category that suits you. Pick the one that works best for your content and add a few relevant tags that others might use when searching. Up to four is a good number.
  • Enter your email address. Don't worry, it's kept private. We only use it to send you your Edit Key and quick updates about your listing, like when someone comments or upvotes it.
  • Click Launch now and keep your key safe. You'll need the Edit Key to make changes or delete your listing later, so copy it and store it in a secure place.

Writing a listing that people actually click

A good listing gets straight to the point: what will this tool do for you? Don't use vague words like "amazing" or "game-changing". Just say what it does. For example, "shrink images in your browser without sending them anywhere" is way more helpful than "the best image tool". It tells you exactly what you get.

You have 400 characters to describe your tool, so make the most of them. Tell us who it's for and what makes it easy to use. Also say whether an account is needed. If it's free and doesn't require sign-up, be sure to mention that. Lots of people look for exactly that, which is why our no-signup tag is so popular. Keep it short and sweet.

A simple logo is a big help. If you don't add one, we'll show a coloured letter instead, which is okay, but a real logo makes your listing look more reliable right away. It's all about trust at first sight.

The seven categories, explained

  • AI Tools: assistants, generators, and summarizers - helpers that use machine learning to get the job done.
  • Developer Tools: formatters, testers, converters, and other utilities for people who write code.
  • Productivity: timers, planners, calculators, and little apps that save you time every day.
  • Marketing: tools for making content, managing social media, tracking results, and finding more customers.
  • Design: image editors, color pickers, mockup helpers, and other creative utilities.
  • Finance: tools for budgeting, loans, taxes, and currency exchange. Always double-check the results before using them to make any big decisions.
  • Education: study aids, tools for students and teachers, and websites with useful reference information.

If your tool belongs to two groups, pick the one that people would check first. Use tags for the rest.

How visitors can discover tools they will love

When you land on the homepage, you'll see the latest launches right up front, organized by day. This way, you can easily check out what's new today and what arrived yesterday. If you want to see what's popular, switch to the Top view - it shows the products that people have voted for. The category tabs tell you how many tools are in each group, and the tags under the search box make it easy to filter with just one tap. Feeling indecisive? Hit "Surprise me" and we'll open a random product for you to check out.

Each product has its own page with more details and links to similar tools. You can browse them all on the All Products page - a straightforward list grouped by category. This makes it easy to find what you're looking for.

Why we keep upvotes and comments simple

To keep things fair, each browser can upvote a product only once. This way, one person can't push something to the top by themselves. Anyone can leave a comment, but links are not allowed. This keeps the conversation useful and stops the comment section from becoming a place for ads.

When someone leaves a comment on your product, you get a quick email. You also get an email when your product reaches certain upvote milestones. Responding to comments is really important after you launch. It's a great way to show that there's a real person behind the product. A simple, friendly reply can turn a first-time visitor into a regular user. It makes a big difference.

Being honest about links and SEO

We're upfront about what you get from being listed here. Every outgoing link from this directory is marked nofollow. This means we don't pass any ranking boost to your website, and we don't let people pay for a better spot on the list. The truth is, no one can guarantee you'll show up in search results, and we're not going to make any false promises.

What you do get is a public page that shows what your tool is all about. You also get to hear what real people think of it, which is really valuable. And you get a link that can send curious people to your site, which is great for new projects. Those first few visitors and comments can be super helpful - sometimes even more helpful than trying to figure out how to get a higher ranking.

What to do after you launch

  • Share your product with friends and post it on social media and in online groups where people might really use it. Just follow the rules of each group and don't send too many messages that could look like spam.
  • Respond to every comment, even if it's just to say thanks.
  • Use your Edit Key to improve your tagline or description as you learn what works best with people.
  • Make sure your website is running smoothly. Visitors can report broken links, and a broken listing may be removed.
  • Launch your next project too. Each new tool is another chance to meet new users.

Your privacy and your Edit Key

We only collect the information we need to make the directory work. Your email address is kept private, and we use it to send you your Edit Key and short updates about activity on your listing. We don't send out newsletters. You can find all the details in simple terms on our Privacy Policy page. If you want to remove a listing, you can ask us anytime through the Contact page.

Your Edit Key is like a password for your product, since there are no accounts. So keep it to yourself, because anyone with it can change or remove your listing. If you misplace it, look for the welcome email we sent you when you launched.

Our achievements and journey

Product Launch Directory is still young, but it is growing step by step. Here is an honest look at what we have built so far and why we built it.

What this directory means

This directory exists for one simple reason: a good free tool should be easy to find, even when its maker has no followers, no budget and no time for complicated sign-ups. Every product listed here was added by its own maker, in about two minutes, without creating an account.

Why we built it

We run Global Tools Box, a free collection of web tools, and we saw how hard it is for a new tool to get its first visitors. Big launch sites are crowded, and a new maker can disappear on the first day. So we built a calmer place where small projects can be seen, upvoted and discussed by real people.

What we have achieved so far

  • More than 330 plus free tools have been launched by makers from different countries.
  • A separate page for every product, so each tool has its own link that can be shared.
  • Seven clear categories: AI Tools, Developer Tools, Productivity, Marketing, Design, Finance and Education.
  • Email notices for makers when someone comments on their product or it reaches upvote milestones.
  • Auto-fill for new launches, which prepares a name, tagline and logo from your website.
  • Community posts with likes and comments, so makers can share short updates.
  • Light and dark mode, search, tags and Surprise me, so discovering tools stays easy on any phone.

What comes next

We plan to add more guides, better ways to discover tools and more ways for makers to get feedback. We add new things slowly and only when they help real users. If you have an idea, tell us on the Contact page.
