A free place to launch your web tool

Product Launch Directory is a simple, free launchpad for makers. If you built a web tool, an app or a small side project, you can list it here in about two minutes. There is no account to create and no password to remember. You paste your website address, check the details and press submit.

Visitors can browse the newest launches, see what is trending and upvote the tools they like. Every product has its own details page with comments, so you can get real feedback from real people, not just a number next to your name.

How it works

Click Submit and enter your website URL. The Auto-fill button reads the public title and description of your site and suggests a name, a tagline and a logo. You can change anything before you send it. When you finish, you get a private Edit Key. Keep it safe: it is the only way to update or delete your product later. We also email you a copy within a few minutes.

What this directory is, and what it is not

We want to be honest with you. Every outgoing link from this directory is nofollow, so please do not expect a ranking boost from it. What you get is visibility, a place to show your work and a chance to meet early users. Nobody can promise search results, and we do not sell positions in the list.

Spam, copied listings and broken links are removed. Each product can be reported by visitors, and we review reports by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to launch my product here?

Yes. Launching is free and there is no sign-up. You only need your website URL, a short description and an email address, which is used to send you your private Edit Key.

How do I edit or delete my product later?

After you submit, you get a private Edit Key. Click Edit with Key, paste it, and you can update or delete your product. A copy of the key is also emailed to you within a few minutes.

Are the links from this directory dofollow?

No. Every outgoing link is nofollow. We do not sell rankings and we cannot promise any SEO result. You get visibility and real visitors, not link juice.

How do upvotes work?

Each browser can upvote a product once. Upvotes help good products move up in the Top view.

What kind of products can I submit?

Free or free-to-try web tools in these categories: AI Tools, Developer Tools, Productivity, Marketing, Design, Finance and Education.
