Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information Product Launch Directory ("we", "us") collects and how we use it. By using this website you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

  • Product listing: name, tagline, category, website, logo URL, tags, X handle and description. This information is shown publicly.
  • Email address: required when you submit a product. It is not shown publicly. We use it only to send you your Edit Key and, if needed, to contact you about your listing.
  • Comments: the name and text you write are shown publicly. Please do not post personal data.
  • Reports: the reason and note you send are used to review a listing.

Information collected automatically

We count product visits and upvotes so that we can show popular products. We do not ask you to create an account. Our server may keep basic technical logs, such as time of a request, to prevent spam and abuse.

Data stored in your browser

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme (light or dark), the products you upvoted, your saved Edit Keys and your comment name. This data stays on your device. You can delete it by clearing your browser data.

Cookies and advertising

This website may show advertisements from third-party networks such as Adsterra. These companies may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure results. We do not control their cookies. You can limit cookies in your browser settings.

We use Google Analytics to count visitors and see which pages are popular. It may set cookies and collects data such as your approximate location, device and pages viewed. We do not use it to identify you personally. You can block it with your browser settings or a privacy extension.

Services we use

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our data is processed with Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. These providers have their own privacy policies.

How we use your information

  • To show and manage product listings.
  • To send you your Edit Key.
  • To prevent spam, abuse and duplicate listings.
  • To improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing newsletters to your email address.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your product at any time with your Edit Key. To ask us to remove your listing or your data, write to us on our Contact page and we will help.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
